# 🎓 Padh Lo - Smart Study Planner

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)
![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-2.0.0-blue)

**Padh Lo** is a high-performance, aesthetically pleasing Smart Study Planner designed to help students master their academic schedule. Built with a focus on productivity, it combines task management, scheduling, and deep analytics into a single, seamless web interface.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📊 Dynamic Dashboard
*   **Focus Timer**: Integrated stopwatch to track your deep work sessions in real-time.
*   **Study Streaks**: Stay motivated with a daily streak counter that tracks your consistency.
*   **Today's Focus**: Intelligent filtering of tasks and schedules due only for today.
*   **Motivational Quotes**: Start your session with curated inspiration to keep you going.

### 📋 Advanced Task Manager
*   **Smart Status Tracking**: Automatic categorization of tasks into *Ongoing*, *Overdue*, and *Completed*.
*   **Visual Feedback**: Completed tasks feature a satisfying strikethrough effect.
*   **Subject Linking**: Every task is tied to a specific subject for better organization.
*   **Deadline Alerts**: Never miss a submission with built-in overdue highlighting.

### 📅 Session Kanban
*   **Kanban Workflow**: Drag-and-drop styled session management.
*   **Conflict Detection**: Intelligent alerts if you try to schedule two sessions at the same time.
*   **Auto-Cleanup**: Past schedules are automatically filtered out to keep your view relevant.

### 📈 Progress Analytics
*   **Visual Insights**: Beautifully rendered charts (via Chart.js) showing task distribution by subject.
*   **Status Overviews**: Stacked bar charts to visualize pending vs. completed work.
*   **Productivity Metrics**: Track overall completion percentages and focus trends.

### 🎓 Academic Tools
*   **GPA Calculator**: Input your marks and weightage to get an instant GPA estimate and grade prediction.
*   **Subject Prioritization**: Order your subjects by priority (Low, Medium, High) to focus on what matters most.

### ⚙️ Personalization & Data
*   **Themes**: Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, and the premium **Vampire** (Glassmorphism) mode.
*   **Typography**: Customize your experience with fonts like *Inter, Poppins, Serif,* or *Mono*.
*   **Data Control**: Full **JSON Export/Import** capabilities and a complete data reset option.
*   **Privacy**: All data is stored locally in your browser (**LocalStorage**). No servers, no tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (Modern Flex/Grid layouts).
*   **Logic**: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+).
*   **Charts**: [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) for data visualization.
*   **Typography**: Google Fonts (Inter, Outfit, Poppins).
*   **Icons**: Native Emojis for a lightweight, zero-dependency experience.
